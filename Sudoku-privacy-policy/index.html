The short version Sudoku does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sudoku mobile application (the “App”). It describes how the App handles information when you use it.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. The App is intended to work offline and does not operate a user account or a backend service.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. In particular, the App does not request or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or payment information.

The App does not include registration, login, user profiles, or a server-side database for gameplay information.

Information Stored Locally

The App may save information on your device so that it can provide its core features. This can include:

  • Game progress, completed puzzles, and scores
  • App preferences, such as sound or display settings

This information remains on your device under the control of your operating system. We cannot access it remotely. Deleting the App generally removes its locally stored data, subject to your device’s normal backup and restore settings.

Internet, Analytics, and Advertising

The App does not require an internet connection for gameplay. We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, tracking technologies, or third-party SDKs that collect personal information.

The App does not track you across apps or websites and does not share information with data brokers or other third parties.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the App may use your device’s notification features to send reminders or other app notifications. You can change notification permissions at any time in your device settings. We do not use notifications to collect personal information.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users. Because no account or personal-data collection is required, the App can be used without providing personal information.

Data Security and Retention

Because gameplay information is stored locally, its security and retention are also affected by your device’s security settings, backups, and access controls. We retain no personal information on our own systems.

Privacy Choices and Data Deletion

You can stop all local processing by deleting the App. You can also manage notification permissions through your device settings. Since we do not maintain user accounts or remote personal-data records, there is no separate account deletion request required.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when the policy was most recently revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Information

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the App’s privacy practices, contact the developer through the support contact listed on the App Store product page.

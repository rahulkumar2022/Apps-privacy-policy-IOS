1. What this app does

Todo: Task Tracker stores your tasks, lists, tags, and settings on your device using on-device storage (Apple SwiftData and UserDefaults). There is no account, no sign-in, and no server.

2. Data we collect

We collect nothing. No analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no tracking, no user profiling. Your tasks never leave your device unless you explicitly export or share them.

3. Notifications

Reminders are delivered as local notifications scheduled on your device. Notification permission is requested only when you enable a reminder. No notification data is sent anywhere.

4. Data you share

If you use Export, the app writes a JSON or CSV file that you choose where to send. The app itself transmits nothing.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting a task removes it immediately; “Delete all tasks” removes every task. Uninstalling the app removes all app data.

6. Children’s privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If the app’s data practices ever change (for example, optional sync is added), this policy will be updated before release and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated to match.
